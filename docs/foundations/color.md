# Foundations — Color

:::color-plates

## Rules

- **C1 (derived — WCAG 1.4.3, HIG Clarity).** Overlaid body text: minimum
  7.0:1 against its backing plate. Rationale: a live reflection behind the
  plate destroys effective contrast; the enhanced 1.4.6 level is the baseline
  here, not the upgrade.
- **C2 (borrowed — Material dark theme).** Prefer light-on-dark plates over
  dark-on-light. A bright plate on a mirror reads as a hole in the glass.
- **C3 (proposed).** Backing plates must be opaque. Translucent plates over a
  moving reflection fail C1 unpredictably with room lighting.
- **C4 (borrowed — WCAG 1.4.1).** Color is never the only signal; pair with
  shape, label, or position.

## Palette guidance

Dark charcoal plates (`#171717` family), white primary text, one accent
reserved for the single primary action per screen. Error red must pass C1 on
the plate, not on paper.
