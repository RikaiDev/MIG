# Principles

1. **P1 The reflection is the content** (derived — HIG Deference). UI defers to
   the user's own face. Nothing persistent may claim the center.
2. **P2 Input is a choice, not a given** (derived). A magic mirror is half-mirror
   plus screen. No input method is mandatory. Pick from the method catalog in
   `interaction-methods.md` and justify the pick against the task, not habit.
3. **P3 Glanceable passing by, operable up close** (derived — HIG
   Clarity, Material honest UI). Close work and passing glances, one layout.
4. **P4 Every method declares its failure modes** (derived — Norman gulfs of
   execution and evaluation). A method you cannot describe failing is a
   method you have not designed.
5. **P5 Calm by default** (derived — Material calm technology, WCAG 2.3.3).
   No motion that is not feedback; no feedback that is not reducible.
