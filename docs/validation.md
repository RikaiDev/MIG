# Validation

A `proposed` rule graduates by evidence, not by vote:

1. **Optical ROI fraction (M1/L2).** Calibrate per panel model (procedure in
   `mirror-hardware.md`). Graduates when three panel models report.
2. **7.0:1 overlay baseline (C1/M2).** Graduates with a 20-user readability
   study under three lightings, or is downgraded to 4.5:1 with data.
3. **Zero-touch parity (A5).** Graduates when two independent tasks complete
   end-to-end by voice alone in user testing.
4. **Presence display (A3).** Graduates on privacy-review sign-off, not on
   usability scores.

Until then: tools cap proposed rules at `info`; humans may overrule upward
with a recorded reason.
